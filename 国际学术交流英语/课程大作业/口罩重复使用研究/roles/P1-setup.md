# P1 · 开场 · 立问题

**建议承担：甲（P1）** ｜ 时长 2.5–3 min ｜ 负责 PPT 第 1–3 页 ｜ 难度 ★☆☆☆☆

> 这段的价值不在信息量，而在**把全场的骨架立住**。第 3 页的"三问"讲清楚，后面三个人就有依靠。稳住语速、别赶。

---

## 完整英文稿（约 300 词）

Good morning, everyone. We are Group [X]. I'm [Name], and today we'd like to talk about a very ordinary object — a disposable face mask — and one very practical question: **can you reuse it?**

In early 2020, this was not a small question. According to China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the country's total mask production was only **8 million a day** on the 23rd of January 2020 — for a population of over **1.4 billion**. The gap between supply and demand was enormous. So the question became urgent: **is there a way to make one mask last longer — safely?**

The paper we chose is called *Can Masks Be Reused After Hot Water Decontamination During the COVID-19 Pandemic?* It was published in 2020 in **Engineering**, the official journal of the Chinese Academy of Engineering. The first author is Dan Wang, from Beijing University of Chemical Technology.

One detail I'd like to point out: the paper was **received on the 26th of May 2020, and accepted three days later**. A three-day review is extremely unusual. That tells you how urgent this problem was at the time.

The **method** they propose is called "hot water decontamination plus charge regeneration." In plain English: **soak the mask in hot water, then dry it with a hair dryer.** No chemicals, no special machines — you can do it in your kitchen.

Now, this paper is essentially a **qualification test**. Before you approve any reuse method, you have to answer three questions. **One: does it kill the virus? Two: does the mask still filter? Three: does it survive real use?**

My teammate [Name] will start with the procedure itself.

## 你认领的 AWL 词（5 个，讲三遍就记住）

| 词 | Sublist | 义 | 论文里的用法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **method** | S1 | 方法 | ...decontaminated by appropriate **methods**? |
| **approach** | S1 | 思路、路子 | ...the hot water **approach** is easy to achieve at home. |
| **evidence** | S1 | 证据【不可数】 | We provide solid **evidence** that... |
| **resources** | S2 | 资源 | ...scarce **resources** cannot meet the demand. |
| **period** | S1 | 时期 | ...over a long **period** of time. |

**写稿任务**：上面这份稿子已经用到了 method / approach / evidence。你再自己加一句带 `resources` 或 `period` 的话（例如背景段加 "production capacity and medical **resources** were stretched"）。

## 发音雷区

- **resources** /rɪˈsɔːsɪz/ — 重音在第二音节，不是 RE-sources
- **method** /ˈmeθəd/ — /θ/ 咬舌
- **approach** /əˈprəʊtʃ/ — 重音在第二音节
- **evidence** /ˈevɪdəns/ — 三音节，别念成 "evi-dence"
- 数字：**8 million** = eight million；**1.4 billion** = one point four billion
- 日期：**the 26th of May** = the twenty-sixth of May（th 咬舌）
