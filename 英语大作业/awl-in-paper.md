# AWL × 口罩论文 · 双效词表

> **核心思路：一张卡干两件事。**
> 左边是你英语老师要求的 AWL 词条，右边是**它在你即将汇报的论文里的真实原句**。
> 你背这个词的同一分钟，也在复习汇报内容、熟悉论文句式、积累可脱口而出的表达。
>
> 标记说明：`S1–S10` = 该词在你《AWL 自学词汇手册》里的 Sublist 编号；`×n` = 在论文中出现次数。
> **当前进度**：全手册 542 词，你已勾 7 个；本篇论文命中 **81 个 AWL 词**，其中 24 个属高频。

## A 组 · 论文高频 24 词（先背这批，性价比最高）

按"论文里出现多 + 汇报时你会亲口说到"双重标准挑出来的。**汇报前必须能脱口而出的一批。**

### 1. layer <sub>S3 · ×27</sub>
*n.* 层
- 原句：Typical masks are composed of three **layers** of non-woven fabric.
- 汇报用法：讲口罩解剖结构时的骨架词。`outer layer / filter layer / inner layer`，三层一次说清。

### 2. method <sub>S1 · ×25</sub>
*n.* 方法
- 原句：How many times can masks be decontaminated by appropriate **methods**?
- 辨析：**method**（成体系的方法） vs **approach**（切入问题的思路/路子） vs **procedure**（可照做的操作步骤）。这篇论文三个词都用了，正好是活教材。

### 3. process <sub>S1 · ×10</sub>
*n.* 过程
- 原句：We also studied the influence of actual service **processing** on masks.
- 汇报用法：`the decontamination process` / `the drying process`，串流程最好用。

### 4. approach <sub>S1 · ×8</sub>
*n.* 方法、思路
- 原句：Thus, the hot water **approach** for mask decontamination is easy to achieve at home.
- 注意：论文里 `approach` 和 `method` 常互换，但 `approach` 更偏"整体路线"，`method` 更偏"具体方法"。

### 5. equipment <sub>S7 · ×7</sub>
*n.* 设备【**不可数名词**，千万别加 s】
- 原句：...compared with approaches that use "high-tech" **equipment** such as UV radiation towers...
- 这是全篇最核心的对比点：**他们的方法不需要高科技 equipment**。必背。

### 6. technical <sub>S3 · ×6</sub> / technology <sub>S3 · ×6</sub>
*adj.* 技术的 / *n.* 技术
- 原句：...several other **technical** solutions for N95 respirators have been reported very recently.
- 辨析：**technical**（技术的、技术性的） vs **technological**（与技术革命/科技水平相关的）。

### 7. cycle <sub>S4 · ×6</sub>
*n.* 循环、周期【**本篇的灵魂词**】
- 原句：We provide solid evidence that the essential performance of the masks was maintained after decontamination for up to 10 **cycles**.
- 你的专业加分点：这就是工程上的**循环次数 / 疲劳寿命**概念。汇报时可以点一句 "Ten cycles — essentially a fatigue-life test for a disposable mask."

### 8. individual <sub>S1 · ×5</sub>
*n./adj.* 个体；个体的
- 原句：For surgical masks, the effects of wearing vary among **individuals** and for the same **individual** at different times.
- 这句话是论文承认数据分散性（variability）的地方，很适合在"局限性"那一页引用。

### 9. significant <sub>S1 · ×5</sub>
*adj.* 显著的；重要的
- 原句：...the regenerated masks should have a **significant** effect in blocking microorganisms...
- ⚠️ **重点陷阱**：学术英语里 `significant` 常指**统计显著**（p < 0.05），不一定是"很大"。这篇论文用它时偏"重要/明显"，但你在别处读论文一定要分辨。

### 10. design <sub>S2 · ×5</sub>
*n./v.* 设计
- 原句：These findings may aid in the development of new materials and **design** strategies for reusable masks.
- 汇报用法：结尾展望时用 `design strategies` 很自然。

### 11. potential <sub>S2 · ×4</sub>
*adj./n.* 潜在的；潜力
- 原句：...allowing a mask to air-dry naturally over a long period of time allows the **potential** growth of a large amount of bacteria.
- 汇报用法：讲"为什么必须用吹风机快速吹干"时，这句就是理由。

### 12. procedure <sub>S1 · ×3</sub>
*n.* 程序、操作步骤
- 原句：Waterproof testing of the masks was performed by a Buchner funnel **procedure**.
- 背诵点：`In a typical procedure, ...` 是方法部分万能开头句。

### 13. mechanism <sub>S4 · ×3</sub>
*n.* 机制
- 原句：The filtering **mechanism** of a typical mask involves Brownian diffusion, entrapment, inertial collision, gravity sedimentation, and electrostatic adsorption.
- 这是**全文最值得背的一句**：一句话五个过滤机制，讲出来非常有专业感。

### 14. retained <sub>S4 · ×3</sub>
*v.* 保持、保留（retain 的过去式）
- 原句：...we found that the masks **retained** a PFE greater than 95%.
- 汇报高频动词：`retain / maintain / preserve` 都表示"性能没掉"，比 keep 学术得多。

### 15. capacity <sub>S5 · ×3</sub>
*n.* 容量；能力
- 原句：The volume of water exceeded 80% of the total **capacity** of the container.

### 16. exceed <sub>S6 · ×3</sub>
*v.* 超过
- 原句：The geometric standard deviation did not **exceed** 1.86.
- 比 `more than` 正式得多，测试方法部分必备。

### 17. furthermore <sub>S6 · ×3</sub>
*adv.* 此外【正式书面语，表递进】
- 原句：**Furthermore**, we extended our investigations to decontamination treatment at a higher temperature.
- ⚠️ 只用于**书面/正式演讲**，日常口语不用。汇报里用一次很显水平。

### 18. evidence <sub>S1 · ×2</sub>
*n.* 证据【**不可数名词**】
- 原句：We provide solid **evidence** that the essential performance of the masks was maintained...

### 19. indicate <sub>S1 · ×2</sub>
*v.* 表明
- 原句：These results **indicate** that the electrostatic charge attenuation velocity of a regenerated mask is similar to that of a new mask.
- 汇报神器：`These results indicate that ...` 讲任何图表都能套。

### 20. demonstrate <sub>S3 · ×1</sub>
*v.* 证明、展示
- 原句：These results **demonstrate** that the regenerated masks should have a significant effect...
- 比 indicate 语气更强一层（indicate = 提示，demonstrate = 证明）。

### 21. contrast <sub>S4 · ×1</sub>
*n./v.* 对比
- 原句：**In contrast**, rapidly regenerating the charge after decontamination prevents the growth of bacteria inside the mask.
- 搭配：**in contrast / by contrast**（相反）；注意别和 **in contrast to / with**（与……形成对比）搞混。

### 22. whereas <sub>S5 · ×1</sub>
*conj.* 然而、而【连接两个对比分句】
- 原句：The standards for single-use medical face masks have no requirement for PFE, **whereas** the standards for surgical masks state that the PFE should be no less than 30%.
- 这一句直接支撑你"批判性加分点"：外科口罩的 PFE 门槛只要 30%。

### 23. enable <sub>S5 · ×1</sub>
*v.* 使能够
- 原句：...a company applied this method to **enable** their workers to extend the use of masks.
- 搭配：**enable sb. to do sth.**

### 24. investigation <sub>S4 · ×1</sub>
*n.* 调查研究
- 原句：**Furthermore**, we extended our **investigations** to decontamination treatment at a higher temperature.

## B 组 · 其余命中词（按 Sublist 顺序，配合你手册的进度推进）

<sub>每条都附论文原句。建议：每天 15 个，遮住中文回忆；已在 A 组出现的不重复。</sub>

### Sublist 1
- **analysis** *n.* 分析 — Waterproof property and microstructure **analysis**...
- **data** *n.* 数据【常作复数：data were】 — ...there is no scientific theory or experimental **data** support to answer these questions.
- **distribution** *n.* 分布 — The particle size **distribution** of the NaCl aerosol...
- **economic** *adj.* 经济的 — ...pollution caused by waste masks is having an adverse effect on the **economic** development...
- **environment** *n.* 环境 — ...an adverse effect on the economic development of society and on the ecological **environment**.
- **factors** *n.* 因素 — Among these **factors**, electrostatic adsorption is critical...
- **function** *n./v.* 功能 — ...to recharge the masks with electrostatic charge to recover their filtration **function**.
- **period** *n.* 时期 — ...allowing a mask to air-dry naturally over a long **period** of time...
- **required** *adj./v.* 必需的 — The company then **required** their workers to apply the method...
- **research** *n.* 研究【不可数】 — ...financial support from the National Key **Research** and Development Program...
- **role** *n.* 作用【play a role in】 — ...the electrostatic charge plays an important **role** in intercepting viruses.
- **similar** *adj.* 类似的【similar to】 — ...is **similar** to that of a new mask.
- **specific** *adj.* 特定的 — ...the PFE should be no less than 95% under **specific** conditions.
- **structure** *n.* 结构 — ...did not damage the **structure** of the polypropylene fibers.
- **theory** *n.* 理论 — ...there is no scientific **theory** or experimental data support...

### Sublist 2
- **achieve** *v.* 达到 — ...is easy to **achieve** at home.
- **appropriate** *adj.* 恰当的 — ...decontaminated by **appropriate** methods?
- **conclusion** *n.* 结论 — **Conclusions**: Masks have been recommended as a powerful tool...
- **conduct** *v.* 进行 — ...have **conducted** active and beneficial exploration.
- **range** *n.* 范围 — ...polypropylene microfibers with diameters in the **range** of 1–10 µm.
- **region** *n.* 地区 — ...in humid **regions** or during humid weather.
- **resources** *n.* 资源 — ...the limited productivity and scarce **resources** cannot meet the demand.
- **strategies** *n.* 策略 — ...new materials and design **strategies** for reusable masks.

### Sublist 3
- **removed** *v.* 移除 — ...the mask was **removed** from the hot water.
- **reaction** *n.* 反应 — ...potential interfacial **reactions** between dirt/oil and the fibers.
- **specified** *adj.* 指定的 — ...for the **specified** test conditions...
- **volume** *n.* 体积；量 — The **volume** of water exceeded 80% of the total capacity...
- **published** *v.* 发表 — **Published** by Elsevier on behalf of the Chinese Academy of Engineering.

### Sublist 4
- **communication** *n.* 交流 — We hope that this short **communication** article can contribute to...
  - 【注意】学术语境下 `short communication` = 短篇通讯（一种论文类型），不是"沟通"。
- **concentration** *n.* 浓度 — ...the **concentration** did not exceed 200 mg·m⁻³.
- **implications** *n.* 影响、启示 — These findings should have important **implications** for the reuse of masks.
- **obvious** *adj.* 明显的 — ...the huge mask demand–supply gap is **obvious**.
- **professional** *adj.* 专业的 — ...**professional** masks...

### Sublist 5
- **generated** *v.* 产生 — The fluorescence signal was **generated** by wastes from the oral cavity...
- **evolution** *n.* 演变 — The **evolution** of static electricity on the masks is revealed.

### Sublist 6
- **enhanced** *adj.* 增强的 — ...if the wettability of the masks was **enhanced** by interfacial reactions...
- **incorporated** *v.* 纳入 — ...**incorporated** into the mask surface...
- **index** *n.* 指数、指标 — ...filterability is one of the most important **indexes** of a mask.
- **revealed** *v.* 揭示 — ...is **revealed**.

### Sublist 7
- **aid** *n./v.* 帮助 — These findings may **aid** in the development of new materials...
- **confirmed** *v.* 确认 — Successful regeneration could be **confirmed** by sprinkling the mask with scraps of paper.
- **media** *n.* 介质【medium 的复数】 — ...the filtration efficiency of filter **media**...
- **transmission** *n.* 传播 — ...preventing the **transmission** of human coronaviruses.

### Sublist 8
- **deviation** *n.* 偏差 — ...the geometric standard **deviation** did not exceed 1.86.
- **exhibit** *v.* 表现出 — ...exhibited a bright blue emission...

### Sublist 9
- **behalf** *n.* 代表【on behalf of】 — Published by Elsevier **on behalf of** the Chinese Academy of Engineering.
- **device** *n.* 装置 — The detection system had a **device** to neutralize the charged particles.
- **duration** *n.* 持续时间 — ...the use of masks may be prolonged...
- **preliminary** *adj.* 初步的 — ...a **preliminary** "regeneration treatment" approach...

## ⚠️ 四个"假命中"，别背错义

| 词 | 论文里的真实含义 | 手册义 |
|---|---|---|
| **grade** | KN95-**grade**（……**级**的），口罩等级 | 等级；成绩 |
| **commission** | National Health **Commission**（国家卫生健康**委**），专有名词 | 委员会；委托 |
| **medical** | **medical** masks（医用口罩），固定搭配 | 医学的 |
| **mental** | 提取错误，原文是 experi-**mental** | 心理的 |

| 阶段 | AWL 任务 | 汇报任务 | 共用动作 |
